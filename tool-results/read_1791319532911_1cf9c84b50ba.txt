   632→   632→Attendance should be treated as a **session**.
   633→   633→
   634→   634→```text
   635→   635→NOT_STARTED
   636→   636→     ↓
   637→   637→CHECKING_IN
   638→   638→     ↓
   639→   639→WORKING
   640→   640→     ↓
   641→   641→CHECKING_OUT
   642→   642→     ↓
   643→   643→COMPLETED
   644→   644→```
   645→   645→
   646→   646→Other states:
   647→   647→
   648→   648→```text
   649→   649→REJECTED
   650→   650→CANCELLED
   651→   651→MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT
   652→   652→```

   656→   656→# 24. Check-In Flow
   657→   657→
   658→   658→This is extremely important.
   659→   659→
   660→   660→```text
   661→   661→Employee selects project
   662→   662→        ↓
   663→   663→App requests camera
   664→   664→        ↓
   665→   665→Employee takes live photo
   666→   666→        ↓
   667→   667→App gets GPS
   668→   668→        ↓
   669→   669→App checks GPS accuracy
   670→   670→        ↓
   671→   671→Send attendance request
   672→   672→        ↓
   673→   673→Backend validates project
   674→   674→        ↓
   675→   675→Backend validates employee
   676→   676→        ↓
   677→   677→Backend validates geofence
   678→   678→        ↓
   679→   679→Backend creates attendance
   680→   680→        ↓
   681→   681→Upload photo
   682→   682→        ↓
   683→   683→Attendance becomes WORKING
   684→   684→        ↓
   685→   685→WebSocket event
   686→   686→        ↓
   687→   687→Admin dashboard updates
   688→   688→```

   765→   765→       ↓

   776→   776→83 meters

   778→   778→Result:

   783→   783→
   784→   784→```text
   785→   785→Distance:

   789→   789→200m

  1542→  1542→
  1543→  1543→This is important because employees may have weak internet.
  1544→  1544→
  1545→  1545→App should maintain local queue:

  1562→  1562→
  1563→  1563→A safe approach:
  1564→  1564→
  1565→  1565→- Check-in can be queued briefly.
  1566→  1566→- Capture GPS/photo metadata locally.
  1567→  1567→- Upload once connectivity returns.
  1568→  1568→- Backend records original capture timestamp separately from server receipt time.
  1569→  1569→- Flag delayed submissions for admin review.
