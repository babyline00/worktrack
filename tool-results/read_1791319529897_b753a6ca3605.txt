   634→```text

   636→     ↓

   656→# 24. Check-In Flow
   657→
   658→This is extremely important.
   659→
   660→```text
   661→Employee selects project
   662→        ↓
   663→App requests camera
   664→        ↓
   665→Employee takes live photo
   666→        ↓
   667→App gets GPS
   668→        ↓
   669→App checks GPS accuracy
   670→        ↓
   671→Send attendance request
   672→        ↓
   673→Backend validates project
   674→        ↓
   675→Backend validates employee
   676→        ↓
   677→Backend validates geofence
   678→        ↓
   679→Backend creates attendance
   680→        ↓
   681→Upload photo
   682→        ↓
   683→Attendance becomes WORKING
   684→        ↓
   685→WebSocket event
   686→        ↓
   687→Admin dashboard updates

   732→# 26. Backend Check-In Validation
   733→
   734→Backend must NOT blindly trust mobile.
   735→
   736→It should validate:
   737→
   738→```text
   739→1. JWT valid?
   740→2. Employee active?
   741→3. Project active?
   742→4. Employee assigned to project?
   743→5. Employee already checked in?
   744→6. Location coordinates valid?
   745→7. GPS accuracy acceptable?
   746→8. Employee inside geofence?
   747→9. Server time?
   748→10. Photo uploaded?
   749→11. Duplicate request?
   750→12. Device/session valid?
   751→```

   755→# 27. Geofence Calculation
   756→
   757→Backend calculates distance:
   758→
   759→```text
   760→Employee GPS
   761→       ↓
   762→Project GPS
   763→       ↓
   764→Haversine Distance
   765→       ↓
   766→Distance <= Radius?
   767→```
   768→
   769→Example:
   770→
   771→```text
   772→Project Radius:
   773→200 meters
   774→
   775→Employee Distance:
   776→83 meters
   777→
   778→Result:
   779→✓ ALLOWED
   780→```
   781→
   782→If:
   783→
   784→```text
   785→Distance:
   786→1.4 KM
   787→
   788→Radius:
   789→200m
   790→
   791→Result:
   792→❌ OUTSIDE_GEOFENCE
   793→```

   869→Use:
   870→
   871→```text
   872→Android
   873→ ↓
   874→Compress
   875→ ↓
   876→Upload
   877→ ↓
   878→Object Storage
   879→ ↓
   880→Database stores URL/key
   881→```

   883→Example storage:
   884→
   885→```text
   886→attendance/
   887→2026/
   888→10/
   889→06/
   890→emp_2585436369/
   891→att_001/
   892→check-in.jpg
   893→check-out.jpg
   894→```
   895→
   896→Database stores:
   897→
   898→```text
   899→photoKey
   900→photoUrl
   901→mimeType
   902→size
   903→capturedAt
   904→```
